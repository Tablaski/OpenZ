# AI 2026 and TeamPlay

The solo deploy screen keeps the existing campaign rules selection. Selecting an opponent in the 2026 row changes the opposing controller and uses the most recently selected campaign rules. Selecting a 1996 difficulty restores the classic controller. The solo preference is independent of the bot assignments in local and online Skirmish. The online host owns those assignments and runs the simulation. Solo scores against the two generations are kept distinct by limiting the existing table to the 1996 opponents.

`ZBot` remains the roster entry and dispatches to the selected controller. Its historical relay, production, targeting and order-round code remains the classic execution path. The only cooperative hook sends its idle list to `ZAiTeamPlay` when another active ally exists. TeamPlay uses game alliance and capture rules and only assigns units owned by its bot. The 1996 coordinator preserves the existing relief and support conventions. The 2026 coordinator works from the modern controller's observed contacts and uses a separate risk and urgency calculation.

`ZAi2026` keeps a per-seat observation, a rolling action budget and staggered tactic and strategy schedules. The scout uses last-observed position and movement with decaying confidence. The planner estimates enemy production capacity from known factories and only reads actual queues and build modifiers for its side and allies. Battle analysis reads effective weapons and the active profile's dodge, threat and boost settings. Native OpenZ exchanges receive no assumed dodge advantage. Z Classic and custom profiles remain sources of estimated benefits through their actual unit overrides.

`ZAiDifficulty` holds the per-level tuning. `ZAiSight` and `ZBattleSight` share radar-loss range and mask operations with the presentation. `ZAiOrders` owns action accounting and passes issued commands to the simulation's order and production interfaces. Waypoint validation, paths, bridge damage, production and victory remain with the engine. TeamPlay and AI 2026 can be ported by wiring the roster dispatcher, sight adapter and these existing game interfaces.

Run the headless checks with `dotnet run --project tests/Multiplayer/Multiplayer.Checks.csproj -c Release`. Set `OZ_AI_2026_CHECKS=1` for the focused set. These checks cover controller creation, reaction windows, deterministic results, grouped action accounting, profile overrides and radar effects. The player validation record lists the remaining manual checks.

Fort timing, the bridge trap and projected outcomes are estimates to calibrate on real matches. The advanced planner uses local exchanges and short forecasts. It can send a cheap or durable unit to draw attention before a fort rush, and only dismounts a cannon crew for a close, observed capture away from the home fort when both the cannon and flag are clear of visible threats.
